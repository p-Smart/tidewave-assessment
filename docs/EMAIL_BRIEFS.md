# Email Briefs

This document contains briefs for the two candidate email templates in Section D.

---

## 1. Street Team Prospect (`street-team-prospect`)

### Audience
Applicants who submitted a street team lead form and scored high enough for screening.

### Goal
Invite the applicant to schedule their initial screening call.

### Tone
Direct, polite, professional, and clear.

### Must-Include Items
- Applicant first name (`{{firstName}}`).
- Explanation of the next step (`{{nextStepText}}`).
- Call-to-action button or link pointing to `{{screeningUrl}}`.
- Clear preheader text visible in inbox previews.
- Clear Tidewave Music disclaimer: applying is not an offer of employment or a binding engagement.
- Unsubscribe link.
- Mobile-friendly layout (readable at 375px width).

### Must-NOT-Say Items
- Do not state or imply employment, hiring, salary, or wages.
- Do not use the words "hired", "active", "active rep", or "field ready".
- Do not guarantee admission to the street team.

## 2. Solace Avenue Campaign Intro (`solace-intro`)

### Audience
Listeners who opted in to hear about Solace Avenue and the Harmattan Sessions campaign.

### Goal
Introduce the campaign and provide a link to learn more and back the project.

### Tone
Warm, music-focused, and straightforward.

### Must-Include Items
- Recipient first name (`{{firstName}}`).
- Link to the campaign page (`{{campaignUrl}}`).
- Clear preheader text.
- Plain statement that backing is entirely voluntary.
- Notice that backing is not an investment, not a loan, and not employment.
- Unsubscribe link.
- Mobile-friendly layout (readable at 375px width).

### Must-NOT-Say Items
- Do not promise financial returns, profit, royalties, equity, or interest.
- Do not claim that backing provides employment or contractual rights.
- Do not create false urgency or aggressive promotional claims.
